What it does

Four things, each of which normally costs an afternoon.

▤

Merge the returns

GSTR-1, 3B, 2A, 2B, 9, 9C, IFF and the e-Way Bill report, for as many periods as you have, stacked into one workbook. Columns are matched by their heading rather than their position, so a change in the portal's export does not silently read the wrong column. Every row carries the file it came from.

⚑

Apply the scrutiny parameters

Thirty parameters drawn from the CBIC scrutiny module, each on its own worksheet with every column headed by the source it reads — 3B 3.1(a) IGST, GSTR-2B Available ITC CGST — so a figure can be checked without a key. Each tax head is tested on its own; an excess in one head is not cancelled by a shortfall in another.

⇄

Match the inward supplies

GSTR-2B against your purchase register, invoice by invoice, with Rule 37 ageing on invoices unpaid beyond one hundred and eighty days and a supplier-wise view of credit from cancelled registrations and non-filers.

◈

Draft the ASMT-10

A reference draft in Word, with the statutory paragraph, the amount head by head, and the month-wise working behind it. Findings are separated into what can be demanded, what needs verification first, and what is reported for information — because they are not the same thing.

What it will not do

A tool that overstates what it knows is worse than no tool.

It will not issue a notice

What Pragyan produces is a reference draft. It carries no file number, no letterhead and no signature. Those belong to your office's own record, and the draft goes on to it before anything is issued.

It will not report nil as a clean chit

Where a statement was never imported, the parameter that reads it says “not tested” and is excluded from every total. A nil figure that only means “we could not look” is the most dangerous output a tool like this can produce.

It will not call a risk a demand

A supplier's registration having been cancelled does not establish that the recipient took the credit. Such populations are marked verification required, kept out of the demand, and put to the taxpayer as a question.

How it works

Four steps. Nothing is written until every return has been read.

1

Load

Drop in the portal downloads — ZIP, XLSX, XLS, CSV or PDF. Pragyan works out what each file is. Anything it could not read is listed, so nothing is built on a file that failed silently.

2

Build

A monthly summary of every figure the parameters rest on, then one worksheet per parameter with live formulas into it. Correct a filing date there and everything moves with it.

3

Check

Each parameter states its provision, what it compares, and any caveat that has to be considered before it is pressed. The workbook flags what it could not test as well as what it found.

4

Draft

A Word reference for ASMT-10 in three sections — prima facie discrepancy, verification required, and information — with the month-wise working under each issue.

Where your data goes

Nowhere.

It runs on your machine

Pragyan is a desktop application. The returns you load, the workbooks it writes and the drafts it produces stay on the computer you run it on.

No account, no upload

There is nothing to sign in to. The application does not transmit returns, GSTINs, taxpayer names or any figure to us or to anyone else.

This website collects nothing

No cookies, no analytics, no tracking scripts, no embedded fonts fetched from a third party. Read the privacy policy.

If you are reporting a wrong figure

It helps enormously to know the parameter number, the tax period, the figure Pragyan produced and the figure you expected. Please do not attach real returns, GSTINs or taxpayer names — a description of the discrepancy is enough to find almost any defect.
