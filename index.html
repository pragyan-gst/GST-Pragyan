The desktop tool

And when the charts aren't enough

GST Pragyan is a desktop application that reads the returns you download from the portal and reconciles them against each other.

▤

Merge the returns

GSTR-1, 3B, 2A, 2B, 9, 9C, IFF and the e-Way Bill report, for as many periods as you have, stacked into one workbook. Columns are matched by their heading rather than their position, so a change in the portal's export cannot silently read the wrong column.

◉

Reconcile and check

Thirty checks, each on its own worksheet with every column headed by the source it reads. Each tax head is tested on its own, so an excess in one head is never cancelled by a shortfall in another.

⇄

Match the purchases

GSTR-2B against your purchase register, invoice by invoice, with Rule 37 ageing on invoices unpaid beyond one hundred and eighty days.

◈

Draft the ASMT-10 reference

A Word draft with the statutory paragraph, the amount head by head and the month-wise working — separated into what is quantified, what needs verification first, and what is only information.

It runs on your own computer. No account, no sign-in, no upload. The returns you load and the workbooks it writes stay on the machine you run it on. Read the privacy policy — it is short.

Read this bit

What it will not do

A tool that overstates what it knows is worse than no tool at all.

✕

It will not decide anything

What it produces is a reference draft. Whether a figure becomes a demand, a reply, or nothing at all is a judgement, and it stays with the person making it.

✕

It will not report nil as a clean chit

Where a statement was never imported, the check that reads it says “not tested” and is excluded from every total. A nil that only means “we could not look” is the most dangerous output a tool like this can give.

✕

It will not call a risk a conclusion

A supplier's registration having been cancelled does not establish that the recipient took the credit. Such figures are marked verification required and kept out of the total.
