The toolkit

Four modules, one clear picture

Each does a job that normally costs an afternoon. Each works on its own.

▤

Merge the returns

GSTR-1, 3B, 2A, 2B, 9, 9C, IFF and the e-Way Bill report, for as many periods as you have, stacked into one workbook. Columns are matched by their heading rather than their position, so a change in the portal's export cannot silently read the wrong column. Every row carries the file it came from.

◉

Apply the scrutiny parameters

Thirty parameters drawn from the CBIC scrutiny module, each on its own worksheet with every column headed by the source it reads — 3B 3.1(a) IGST, GSTR-2B Available ITC CGST. Each tax head is tested on its own, so an excess in one head is never cancelled by a shortfall in another.

⇄

Match the inward supplies

GSTR-2B against your purchase register, invoice by invoice, with Rule 37 ageing on invoices unpaid beyond one hundred and eighty days, and a supplier-wise view of credit from cancelled registrations and non-filers.

◈

Draft the ASMT-10

A reference draft in Word with the statutory paragraph, the amount head by head, and the month-wise working behind it — separated into what can be demanded, what needs verification first, and what is reported for information.

What it will not do

A tool that overstates what it knows is worse than no tool at all.

✕

It will not issue a notice

What Pragyan produces is a reference draft. No file number, no letterhead, no signature — those belong to your office's own record, and the draft goes on to it before anything is issued.

✕

It will not report nil as a clean chit

Where a statement was never imported, the parameter that reads it says “not tested” and is excluded from every total. A nil figure that only means “we could not look” is the most dangerous thing a tool like this can produce.

✕

It will not call a risk a demand

A supplier's registration having been cancelled does not establish that the recipient took the credit. Such populations are marked verification required, kept out of the demand, and put as a question.

How it works

Four steps. Nothing is written until every return has been read.

1

Load

Drop in the portal downloads — ZIP, XLSX, XLS, CSV or PDF. Pragyan works out what each file is, and lists anything it could not read.

2

Build

A monthly summary of every figure the parameters rest on, then one worksheet per parameter with live formulas into it.

3

Check

Each parameter states its provision, what it compares, and the caveat to weigh before it is pressed — including what it could not test.

4

Draft

A Word reference for ASMT-10 in three sections, with the month-wise working under each issue.
