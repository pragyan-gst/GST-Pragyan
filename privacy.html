Privacy policy

The short version: Pragyan runs on your computer, keeps your data there, and this website collects nothing about you.

Last updated: 16 August 2026

In one paragraph. The Pragyan desktop application runs entirely on your own computer. The returns you load into it, the workbooks it writes and the drafts it produces never leave that machine. There is no account, no sign-in, no licence check and no telemetry. This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and loads nothing from a third party. If you email us, we hold that email and nothing more.

1. Who this policy is from

Pragyan is an independent working aid for GST return scrutiny, published by its author and contactable at pragyan.gst@gmail.com. It is not published by, endorsed by or affiliated with the Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs, the GST Network, or any Government of India body.

This policy covers two separate things — the desktop application and this website — and they are addressed separately, because they behave quite differently.

2. The desktop application

2.1 What it processes

When you use Pragyan you give it files you have downloaded from the GST portal or prepared yourself. These typically contain a taxpayer's GSTIN, trade name, invoice particulars, supplier and recipient details, and tax figures. Under Indian data protection law such information may be personal data, and in the hands of a departmental officer it is also official information.

2.2 Where that data goes

Nowhere. Pragyan is a desktop application. It reads the files you point it at, writes its workbooks and drafts to a folder you choose, and does nothing else with them. Specifically, the application:

  • does not upload returns, invoices, GSTINs, trade names or any figure to us or to any server;
  • does not require an account, a sign-in, a licence key or an activation;
  • does not send usage statistics, crash reports or telemetry;
  • does not record the identity of the officer using it — the proper officer's name, the office address, the file number and the case register were all removed from the application deliberately, because the office's own record is where those belong;
  • does not need an internet connection to do any of its work.

The consequence is worth stating plainly: we never see your data, and we could not produce it if we were asked to.

2.3 What stays on your computer

Pragyan writes the workbooks and drafts you ask for, to the location you choose. It may also keep small local files such as your last-used folder or your preferred settings. All of it sits on your machine, under your control, and is removed when you delete it. You remain responsible for that data — for where you store it, who can reach it, and the departmental instructions that govern it.

2.4 Security

Because nothing is transmitted, the security of the data is the security of the computer it is on. Follow your office's instructions on official data: keep the machine under your control, use full-disk encryption where your department provides it, and do not place workbooks containing taxpayer particulars on personal cloud storage or unencrypted removable media.

3. This website

3.1 What it collects

Nothing. This site is a set of static pages. It has:

  • no cookies of any kind, including analytics or preference cookies;
  • no analytics — no Google Analytics, no pixel, no tag manager;
  • no advertising and no tracking scripts;
  • no embedded fonts, maps, videos or widgets loaded from a third party, so no third party learns that you visited;
  • no contact form, no comments, no newsletter sign-up;
  • no login and no user accounts.

Because there is no contact form, the only way to reach us is by email, which you initiate — see section 4.

3.2 The calculator on the reference page

The late fee and interest calculator on the GST compliance reference page runs entirely in your browser using a small script served from this site. The dates and figures you type in are never sent anywhere and are not stored; close the tab and they are gone.

3.3 Server logs

This site is served by a third-party static host. Like any web server, that host may keep short-lived technical logs — typically the IP address, the time of the request, the page requested and the browser's user-agent string — for security and to keep the service running. We do not control those logs, do not analyse them, and do not combine them with anything else. The host's own privacy notice governs them. If this matters to you, a VPN or a privacy-respecting browser will address it, and nothing on this site needs your IP address to work.

4. If you email us

If you write to pragyan.gst@gmail.com we receive your email address, your message, and whatever you choose to include. We use it to reply to you and to fix what you report — nothing else. We do not add you to a mailing list, and we do not pass your address to anyone.

That mailbox is hosted by Google, so Google's terms and privacy policy apply to mail in transit and at rest. Messages are kept while they are useful for support and for tracking a defect, and deleted when they are not. You can ask us to delete your correspondence at any time by writing to the same address.

Please do not email us real return data. If you are reporting a wrong figure, tell us the parameter number, the tax period, the figure Pragyan produced and the figure you expected. That is almost always enough to find the defect. Do not attach returns, invoices, GSTINs or taxpayer names — sending official taxpayer information to a third party may also breach your own departmental instructions.

5. Children

Pragyan is a professional tool for tax administration and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under eighteen.

6. Your rights

Data protection law gives you rights over personal data held about you — to know what is held, to have it corrected, and to have it deleted. In our case the answer is short: the only personal data we hold is the email correspondence you have sent us, because the application transmits nothing and the website collects nothing. Write to pragyan.gst@gmail.com and we will tell you what we have or delete it, as you prefer.

Where you use Pragyan on official data in the course of your duties, the department — not us — is the controller of that data, and its own rules and instructions govern how it may be used, stored and shared.

7. Links to other sites

This site links to the CBIC GST portal and may link to other official resources. Those sites have their own privacy policies and we are not responsible for them.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published here with a new date at the top. If a change ever means the application or the site begins collecting something — which is not planned — it will be stated prominently and not buried in a revision.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy, about what Pragyan does with data, or a request to delete your correspondence: pragyan.gst@gmail.com.

No warranty. Pragyan is provided as a working aid, without warranty of any kind. Everything it produces is a reference draft. Every figure must be verified against the return, and every legal position against the Act, the Rules and the notifications in force, before it is put to a taxpayer. Nothing on this site or in the application is legal advice.
